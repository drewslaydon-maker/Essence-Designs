A reading

It is standing at the place where the path bends toward the marsh. It is standing the way people stand when they have forgotten what they were going to do next.

Its hands are open. One of them is holding something — a small, dented tin cup, with a chip out of the rim. The cup is the kind a person would keep for a long time. The cup is being held in the way a person holds a thing they have just picked up again and do not yet remember the use of.

It is not looking at you. It is looking at the cup. Its shoulders move with its breath, and the breath is the loudest thing.
