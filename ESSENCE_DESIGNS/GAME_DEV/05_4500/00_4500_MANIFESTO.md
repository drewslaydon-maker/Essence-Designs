Refined; Re-finding humanity

# 4500 

is a solo far-future post-nuclear post-society rebuilding of humanity. People must re-find and refine their place in the world. Focus on Plant life, Animals, and grant mind bending events. Something like FALLOUT with a world similar to Horizon: Zero Dawn, and elements of society building games, but with a tribal, effectively ZERO start for us. Also something like Death Stranding would be a huge influence on narrative, and world direction. Nier Automata as well. Would likely be the last game on our release state
