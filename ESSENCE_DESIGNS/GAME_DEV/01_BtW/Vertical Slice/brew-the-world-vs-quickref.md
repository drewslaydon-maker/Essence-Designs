## Blood Family (home life — present & interactive)

### Fennel — Protagonist · Botanical
- **Want:** Keep shop/family alive without becoming Cedar
- **Fear:** Complicity past no-return
- **Secret:** Unknowing literal supply engine for the war
- **Color:** Feathery yellow-green
- **Silhouette:** Botanical grammar (flowing, organic) — but as a working shopkeeper, scaled down to a single botanical embroidery detail on her apron, not full robes. Marks her tier inside a mundane occupation.

### Cedar — Father, quartermaster · Botanical, sphere-swing (full — The Heat, The Emperor)
- **Want:** Protect family via compliance
- **Fear:** Repeating what broke him
- **Secret:** Recruited by Hemlock in person at Sage's bedside — never told her which man got there first
- **Color:** Warm reddish wood-brown against evergreen
- **Silhouette:** Botanical grammar, but the swing-ambiguity costume tell lives here specifically — requisition coat either sharply maintained (order) or slightly too careful, like hiding fraying at the seams (repeating what broke him). Worth playing deliberately ambiguous even in the VS.

### Sage — Mother, resistance healer · Botanical
- **Want:** Undo harm at the root
- **Fear:** Losing another child to the machine
- **Secret:** Full active Weeds operative; trained under Marigold; loved Weasel before Cedar
- **Color:** Dusty grey-green (the name already is the color)
- **Silhouette:** Botanical grammar — flowing, layered, sage-sprig embroidery at collar.

### Kirkwood — **Absent Day 1** (deployed, returns ~Act 3) · Brother, broadcaster · Botanical, sphere-swing (full — The Steam, Judgement)
- **Bridge:** not physically present or in the interactive `CHARACTERS`/`DIALOGUE` data. His absence should register in the Home scene (an empty place at the table, or an acknowledgment line from another family member — who carries that line is still an open call, see the VS plan doc) rather than passing without comment.
- **Want:** To matter — to tell what actually happened
- **Fear:** Fading to nobody; that his war supply chain traces to Fennel's shop
- **Secret:** Owl's protégé, secondhand; carries what service really looked like filtered into what airs
- **Color:** Not yet individually specified — flag for style-bible follow-up before he needs full sprite treatment when he actually appears (Act 3, not Day 1)
- **Silhouette:** Botanical grammar (general) — no individual sprite notes locked yet.

### Clover — Younger sibling · Botanical
- **Want:** Simple, present-tense things
- **Fear / Secret:** None, by design
- **Color:** Plain bright grass-green
- **Silhouette:** Botanical grammar.

### Daisy — Younger sibling, sweetheart tone · Botanical
- **Want / Fear:** None, by design
- **Secret:** What Robin is trying to protect — the uncomplicated warmth at stake in his Sparrow dilemma
- **Color:** White and yellow, legibly the "purity" palette
- **Silhouette:** Botanical grammar.
