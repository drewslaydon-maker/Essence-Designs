\#### Read-aloud



The air grows warmer with every step.



The stale smell of abandoned stone slowly gives way to burning iron.



Beyond the final archway...



...lies a forge unlike anything you've ever seen.



A vast circular chamber stretches before you.



Ancient machinery lines the walls.



Rivers of molten metal glow beneath cracked stone walkways.



Two enormous pillars stand watch over a silent central forge, untouched for generations.



Yet...



something here still lives.



At first you hear only the slow creak of old chains.



Then...



one heavy footstep.



\*\*THOOM.\*\*



Dust falls from the ceiling.



Another.



\*\*THOOM.\*\*



From beside the forge, a massive armored figure slowly rises.



Steel grinds against stone.



Molten light seeps through cracks in ancient armor that has not moved in centuries.



The giant rests an enormous forge hammer against the floor...



...and turns its head toward you.



Twin embers ignite where its eyes should be.



It does not roar.



It does not threaten.



It simply speaks.



\*\*"...Unauthorized presence detected."\*\*



A long pause.



\*\*"...Forge protocol remains active."\*\*



The hammer lifts.



\*\*"...I... remain... the Last Warden."\*\*

\#### 🎞 Closing Scene



The forge falls silent.



For the first time in centuries...



...nothing moves.



The Last Warden remains standing for a long moment, his great hammer still planted against the stone.



Then...



the orange glow beneath his armor begins to dim.



One by one, the ancient seams of molten light fade into darkness.



His grip loosens.



The hammer slips from his hand with a deafening crash that echoes throughout the mine.



He slowly lowers himself to one knee.



Ancient gears groan.



Stone cracks beneath his weight.



His voice, once cold and mechanical... is quieter now.



\*\*"...Threat..."\*\*



A pause.



\*\*"...resolved."\*\*



Another.



\*\*"...Forge..."\*\*



His head lowers.



\*\*"...secure."\*\*



The great construct sits motionless.



As though he has finally allowed himself to rest.



The forge around you begins to change.



The relentless grinding of forgotten machinery comes to an end.



Poisonous air escapes through vents that have remained sealed for generations.



The oppressive weight hanging over the city begins to lift.



Dust settles.



Silence returns.



Not the silence of death...



But of peace.



Among the fallen stone, something small catches your eye.



Thick black oil slowly gathers near the forge where the Warden fell.



At first, it pools without purpose.



Then...



it begins to move.



Tiny paws press into the soot.



A small black creature pulls itself free, blinking at the world as if seeing it for the very first time.



It looks around the silent chamber...



...then quietly walks toward you.



It nudges your boot.



Tilts its head.



And waits.



AS PLAYERS LEAVE THE DUNGEON



Outside the forge, the old memorial tablets that once stood cracked and forgotten now catch the light.



For the first time in centuries...



The names carved into the stone are readable again.
