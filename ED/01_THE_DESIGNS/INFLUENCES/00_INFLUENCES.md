# INFLUENCES

*The media, mechanics, math, and psychology that inspire ESSENCE DESIGNS.*

---

## 🎮 Mechanics & Math
- **Balatro:** The elegance of multiplicative stacking, joker synergy, and immediate mathematical feedback. (Heavily influenced PROSIS personal mitigation stacking).
- **Jackbox:** The asymmetry of party games, hidden information, and social deduction. (The core driver for The Boardroom).
- **Hades:** Narrative progression tied directly to failure states. Entropy as a mechanic, not a punishment.
- **Slay the Spire:** Perfecting the readable UI. The player should always know exactly how much damage is coming, eliminating unfair surprise in favor of tactical crisis management.

## 📺 Media & Narrative Structure
- **Mob Psycho 100:** The philosophy that having special powers doesn't make you inherently better than anyone else. Emotional intelligence over brute strength. (Halloween 2026 Cosplay Target).
- **Hideo Kojima / Metal Gear:** Fourth-wall breaking, deeply philosophical meta-narratives masquerading as action games. (Influences Stage & Saddle).
- **David Lynch:** Surrealism, dream logic, and the intentional withholding of absolute answers to create sustained atmospheric tension.
- **Hunter x Hunter (Nen System):** The ultimate expression of personalized magic systems where restrictions and vows define the ceiling of power, not just raw stats. (The absolute foundation of Heaven's Nen Arena).

## 🧠 Player Psychology
- *"Enable and allow. Do not punish. Restrict within reason. Fun is the greatest truth, and so is math."*
- **Sisyphus Theme:** Entropy is an inevitability, not a villain to defeat. The goal is to survive and manage the decline, rather than achieve impossible perfection.
