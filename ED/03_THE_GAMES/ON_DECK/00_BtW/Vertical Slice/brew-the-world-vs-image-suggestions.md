# BREW the WORLD — VS Character Visual Suggestions

*For the codex image pass. Not final art direction — reference points and framing notes to guide what you pick or commission for each Day 1 character. Ties back to the style bible's color language, sprite silhouette grammar, and lighting rules so anything you add stays consistent with what's already locked. Covers the same 17-character VS roster as the quick-reference sheet.*

\---

## General reminders before picking images

* **Diorama effect, not portrait realism.** Per the style bible's Octopath anchor — everything should read as composed, a little too perfect, gorgeous in a way that's quietly suspicious. Avoid references that read as gritty/realistic; lean toward warm, curated, slightly theatrical staging even for "plain" characters like Loam or Millet.
* **Botanical tier gets the gentlest, most painterly light** — even in flat reference images, favor soft directional warmth over harsh contrast for Fennel, Cedar, Sage, Kirkwood, Clover, Daisy, Marigold, Mugwort. This is the tier where lighting *is* characterization.
* **Category silhouette grammar first, individual detail second.** If you're stuck choosing between two reference images for a food/animal/elemental character, pick whichever one more clearly reads its category shape language (round-and-soft for food, literal-animal-feature for animal, angular-and-tool-worn for elemental) over one that's just prettier.
* **Color is locked for most of this roster** — see each entry below. For the four still-open colors (Kirkwood, Falcon, Millet, Ferret) and Weasel's deliberately-unresolved one, proposals are marked accordingly; treat those as adjustable, not final, when you're picking references.
* **The swing-ambiguous costume detail matters for sphere-swing characters** — Cedar, Kirkwood, Weasel, Barley all have (or should get) one wearable element that could read two ways. Worth looking for reference images with *some* asymmetry or wear rather than a pristine, single-note outfit.

## Blood Family

### Fennel

* **Look for:** feathery, soft yellow-green botanical references (fennel fronds specifically — the actual plant's wispy texture is the whole visual idea)
* **Pose/expression to capture:** mid-task, not posed — hands busy at the counter or with the scythe mid-swing, caught between domestic and dangerous. Her tension is "capable and about to be in over her head," not heroic.
* **Framing:** warmest light in the game, per style bible — she should look like she belongs in every space she's in, which is part of what makes her secret land later.
* **Locked costume detail:** single botanical embroidery motif on an otherwise plain shopkeeper's apron — look for reference where one small ornamental detail interrupts an otherwise utilitarian outfit.

### Cedar

* **Look for:** cedar wood grain against evergreen — warm reddish-brown wood tones, not a generic brown.
* **Pose/expression:** something held very carefully — not stiff-at-attention, but a controlled stillness that could read as discipline or as someone bracing.
* **Framing:** botanical warm light, but consider slightly cooler/harder shadow at the edges — same "Orchard cold intruding on village warm" logic the style bible uses for Hemlock, just subtler, since Cedar's compliance is the quieter version of the same idea.
* **Locked costume detail:** the requisition coat — deliberately ambiguous, sharply maintained *or* slightly too careful/fraying. Pick a reference that could plausibly be read either way.

### Sage

* **Look for:** dusty grey-green — actual sage leaf color, muted rather than vibrant.
* **Pose/expression:** hands-focused, mid-remedy — healer's competence, warmth with an edge of watchfulness.
* **Framing:** botanical warm light.
* **Locked costume detail:** sage-sprig embroidery at the collar.

### Kirkwood

* **Look for:** no literal plant referent exists for this name (it's a compound, not a species) — proposal is weathered driftwood-grey-brown, warmer at the core (collar/inner layers), paling outward. Look for wood-tone references with a visible warm-to-pale gradient rather than a flat single tone.
* **Pose/expression:** broadcaster posture — practiced ease that doesn't quite reach his eyes. Something performative layered over something not performed at all.
* **Framing:** botanical-tier warm light, but he's the character most likely to physically appear in a colder Orchard-adjacent space (the broadcast platform) — worth having one reference in warm village light and one in a harder institutional light to see how the gradient color idea reads in both.
* **Note:** color is a proposal, not locked — flag any reference that makes this idea click or fall flat.

### Clover

* **Look for:** plain, bright grass-green — literally clover-colored, no complexity needed by design.
* **Pose/expression:** present-tense, unguarded — she's the one character allowed to just look like a kid.
* **Framing:** botanical warm light, simplest treatment in the family tier.

### Daisy

* **Look for:** white-and-yellow, "purity" palette per style bible — actual daisy coloring.
* **Pose/expression:** sweetheart tone — soft, open, uncomplicated warmth (this is literally what Robin's fear is about losing, so the image should earn that stakes).
* **Framing:** botanical warm light.

## Chosen Family — Hunting Party

### Fox

* **Look for:** rust-orange, actual fox coloring.
* **Pose/expression:** outdoor-capable ease with a guardedness underneath — freedom that's costing him something he won't name.
* **Framing:** less interior-warm than the family tier — he belongs more to the treeline/foraging-path environment than the shop.
* **Locked costume detail:** weathered leather, fur-trim wraps, built for outdoors over the shop counter.

### Mugwort

* **Look for:** silvery-green, faintly otherworldly — actual mugwort has that silvery underside quality, worth leaning into.
* **Pose/expression:** unselfconscious practice — caught mid-ritual or mid-focus, not performing for anyone, since "without anyone's permission or witness" is her entire want.
* **Framing:** god-ray/light-shaft treatment reserved specifically for her scenes per the style bible (Star-card tarot position) — if you can find or stage a reference with a soft light-shaft quality, it'll match the intended treatment better than flat lighting.
* **Locked costume detail:** botanical grammar despite non-family status (same exception as Rose/Branch/Yew) — flowing, embroidered, her own plant motif.

### Falcon

* **Look for:** proposal is slate blue-grey back, rust/copper breast accent — actual peregrine falcon coloring. Not locked; flag if a reference changes your mind.
* **Pose/expression:** watchful stillness, scout posture — someone used to being the quiet one who notices everything.
* **Framing:** animal-tier earthier, muted, naturalistic light — no special treatment yet, he's a Day 1 background-weight character relative to Fox/Mugwort in the trio.

## Craft-Family Peers

### Coal

* **Look for:** part of the Ash/Ember/Coal forge trio — black with ember-undertone specifically (the trio splits pale soot-grey / glowing orange-red / black-ember across the three siblings).
* **Pose/expression:** apprentice concentration — trying hard, a little unsure, proud of small competence.
* **Framing:** elemental-tier harder light, forge environment cues if possible (soot, warm ember glow against grey).
* **Locked costume detail:** soot-marked smith apron, small metal-band accessory (ring/cuff) as the family motif instead of fabric/ribbon.

### Millet

* **Look for:** proposal is pale, dusty grain-gold — lighter and drier than Barley's wheat-gold, since millet the grain is smaller/paler than barley. Not locked.
* **Pose/expression:** market-stall competence trying to look like more than chores — she wants responsibility, so give her something slightly beyond her current role in posture.
* **Framing:** food-tier warm, appetite-coded light.

### Ferret

* **Look for:** proposal is warm sable-brown with a cream underbelly accent — actual ferret coloring. Not locked.
* **Pose/expression:** eager-to-please, slightly too available — someone used to being sent places and telling himself the errands are just errands.
* **Framing:** animal-tier earthier, muted light.

## Craft-Family Faces

### Badger

* **Look for:** black-and-white banding, read literally — actual badger stripe pattern.
* **Pose/expression:** working-hands competence, dignity through craft — mid-forge, not posed for a portrait.
* **Framing:** animal-tier light, forge environment.
* **Locked costume detail:** striped forge apron and gloves as the literal banding motif.

### Loam

* **Look for:** rich earth-brown, actual soil/loam tone.
* **Pose/expression:** provider's tiredness that doesn't quite show — someone doing more math in their head than their face lets on (the skim secret).
* **Framing:** elemental-tier harder light, market/farmwork environment.
* **Locked costume detail:** sturdy, soil-toned farmwear.

### Weasel

* **Look for:** proposal is slate-grey/ink-black base with a thin gold ledger-line accent — deliberately breaking the animal tier's natural earthtone rule, since his whole silhouette point is "office-work dropped into a farming village." Look for cold, non-natural, money-adjacent reference images rather than anything literally weasel-colored.
* **Pose/expression:** composed, transactional — but look for one reference with a flicker of something warmer underneath (Sage), since his leniency toward Fennel is the crack in an otherwise cold presentation.
* **Framing:** the one character who should look slightly wrong in the village-warm environment grading — worth a reference where the lighting doesn't quite match the surroundings.
* **Locked costume detail:** sleek, fitted, vest-and-ledger silhouette.

## Ambient / Background Presence

### Marigold

* **Look for:** saturated gold-orange, hearth-warm — actual marigold coloring.
* **Pose/expression:** whole-town competence — the person everyone brings problems to, visibly carrying more than she shows.
* **Framing:** botanical warm light, clinic environment (per style bible, dressed with small inconsistencies a returning player would notice — worth a reference with something *slightly* off in an otherwise cozy healer's space).
* **Locked costume detail:** flowing robes, marigold-bloom embroidery at collar/cuff.

### Barley

* **Look for:** warm wheat-gold, actual barley coloring.
* **Pose/expression:** **Day 1 specific — present him as an enforcer who genuinely believes what he does is mercy, not yet tested.** No crux content should leak into his VS-stage reference; look for calm, procedural, even kind-seeming rather than menacing.
* **Framing:** the one place food-tier softness and Orchard-institutional coldness should visibly collide — his uniform cut is Orchard-issue, but texture/color underneath is food-tier soft. Worth a reference where the uniform silhouette and the warmer coloring feel slightly at odds with each other.
* **Locked costume detail:** Orchard enforcer uniform cut with food-category softness underneath — visual proof he hasn't fully hardened into the role.
