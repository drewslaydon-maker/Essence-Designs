## 2. WORLD & REGIONAL LORE

### The Docks of Delain
* **Geography:** A lawless coastal sprawl situated directly outside the colossal stone fortifications of **The Greatest City**.
* **Atmosphere:** Wet salt mist, rotting pier pilings, flickering whale-oil lanterns, black harbor water, and ambient sounds of creaking riggings.
* **Culture:** A sanctuary for deserters, black-market arcanists, ex-corsairs, and dissidents fleeing the totalitarian reach of the High Citadel.

### The Venue: "The Greedy Greens"
* **Nature:** A battered, lively pirate dive bar converted from a dry dock warehouse, owned and fiercely defended by Bozog.
* **Layout (Vertical Slice 640x360 Diorama):**
  * **The Reinforced Oak Entrance (South):** Double-planked oak doors bound in corroded iron, leading out to the Delain piers. (Site of the Inciting Ambush).
  * **The Main Bar Counter (West):** Notched mahogany drenched in dark ale and glowing moon-casks. Site of the drinking contest with Atreyu.
  * **The Ramshackle Stage (North-East):** Uneven planks draped in frayed tapestries where Kairon plays chaotic sets to a loyal fringe audience.
  * **The Shadowed Corner Booth (North-West):** Obscured by smoked glass and hanging lanterns where Matchstick studies room reflections.

## 3. THE CANON 4-MAN PARTY

### 1. Bozog the Berserker (The Host & The Hammer)
* **Archetype:** Orc ex-sailor turned corsair, turned dive-bar proprietor.
* **Psychology:** Fueled by Eren Yeager-level conviction regarding personal and collective freedom, matched with a massive, boisterous ego. Craves destruction when it tests raw strength or shatters authority.
* **Visual Representation:** Broad-shouldered green silhouette, rolled-up pirate cuffs, heavy leather breeches, twin tusks, carrying an oversized double-bitted greataxe hooked behind the bar.
* **Mechanical Role:** High-impact kinetic melee, knockback vectors, and the **Rage/Momentum Meter** (gained through combat trades and spent on crushing strikes).
* **Current State:** Active player-controlled avatar exploring the bar **SOLO**.

### 2. Kairon the Eternal (The Chaotic Chronicler)
* **Lineage:** **Hell-blood** (an ancient, singular entity—neither mortal tiefling nor common fiend, but an ageless survivor of forgotten eras).
* **Psychology:** Ozzy Osbourne rock-legend energy. His mind is an overstuffed, disorganized filing cabinet where centuries of historical events, ancient grudges, and musical riffs collide. Universally recognized: either worshipped as a sonic pioneer or dismissed as an erratic madman.
* **Visual Representation:** Swept-back weathered horns wire-wrapped in silver, tattered plum-velvet coat, tinted wire spectacles, carrying an ornate dueling rapier and a tuned repeating hand-crossbow.
* **Mechanical Role:** Harmonic buffs, tempo alteration, dissonance debuffs. *(Late Act 1 Progression: Commands the "Bony Boys" skeleton rhythm section).*
* **Current State:** Performing an erratic solo on stage to a small group of mesmerized patrons.

### 3. Matchstick (The Outside Thinker)
* **Archetype:** Half-elf outlander, rogue scout, and future Mirror Master.
* **Psychology:** Aloof, introspective, laconic. Constantly questions assumptions, steps outside established lines of thought, and gets hypnotically lost in mirror reflections.
* **Visual Representation:** Slender build in a worn leather outlander duster, cropped hair, perpetually spinning a polished silver pocket mirror between his knuckles.
* **Mechanical Role:** High-evasion flanker, shadow relocation, critical strikes. *(Act 1 Finale: Unlocks dimensional mirror relics).*
* **Current State:** Observing the bar floor from the corner booth, reading room angles.

### 4. Atreyu (The High Moon Priest)
* **Archetype:** Dual-aspect lunar arcanist.
* **Psychology:** Serene spiritual devotion coupled with an unruly, fiercely competitive streak. As comfortable slugging heavy grog in a dive bar as she is chanting lunar rites.
* **Visual Representation:** Flowing dark iridescent robes with luminous silver trim, floating crystal shards revolving around her forearms, glowing pale crest on her collar.
* **Mechanical Role:** Dual-Phase Lunar Arts — **Waxing Light** (crystal barriers, regenerative wards) and **Waning Dark** (beam lances, spatial blinds).
* **Current State:** Locked in an intense drinking contest with Bozog at the main counter.

## 5. CORE MECHANICS & SYSTEMS ARCHITECTURE

### The Triad Gameplay Balance
1. **Deltarune Flow:** Responsive 4-directional grid/free movement, typewriter dialogue box with distinct audio beeps per character, comedic/punchy interaction inspects.
2. **D&D Skill Challenges:** Real-time and turn-based tests featuring dynamic target DCs and character trait bonuses.
3. **FF7 Cinematic Scale:** Rich diorama composition, dramatic camera holds, modular character equipment, and impactful standoff phase transitions.

### The 3-Round Drinking Contest Mechanic
* **Interaction:** Bozog speaks to Atreyu at the counter and accepts the challenge.
* **Loop:**
  * **Round 1:** Warmup Ale (Low decay rate, easy threshold).
  * **Round 2:** Docks Rum (Medium decay rate, Atreyu counter-mashes).
  * **Round 3:** Moon-Cask Spirit (Rapid decay, frantic mashing).
* **Controls:** Rapidly mash `[Z]` or `[SPACE]` to fill the tension bar.
* **Smack-Talk Triggers:** Dynamic dialogue bubbles fire between Bozog and Atreyu at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% tension marks.
* **Outcome:** Winning awards party morale and unlocks unique dialogue; failing dazes Bozog with a temporary sway effect.

### The Party Train Transition
* **Rule:** Bozog walks alone during the prologue exploration.
* **Trigger:** Following the Black Knight standoff, the four party members form a pact to demand restitution for the trashed bar.
* **Unlock:** The party locks into a 4-man follow-train (Bozog -> Matchstick -> Atreyu -> Kairon) using an array history buffer to march out onto the docks.

## 7. ENGINEERING & WEB PERFORMANCE AXIOMS
* **Zero Allocations in Loops:** No `new Vector()` or object creation inside the 60 FPS update/render pipeline. Reuse pre-allocated state objects.
* **Trauma Shake Formula:** Screen shake must use quadratic trauma decay: `offset = max_angle * (trauma^2) * sin(time)`.
* **Relative Assets:** Every sprite, font, or audio call must use relative paths (`./assets/...`) to ensure immediate compatibility with GitHub Pages repository URLs.
