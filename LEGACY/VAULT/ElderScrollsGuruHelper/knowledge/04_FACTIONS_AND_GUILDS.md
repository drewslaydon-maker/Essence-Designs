# 04 — FACTIONS, GUILDS & QUESTLINES (PROGRESSION MAP)

> Guilds gate strong gear, trainers, gold, and teleports. Promote by doing
> quests; promotion usually requires certain skill levels on top of quests.
> Exact thresholds vary — treat all rank requirements as `[VERIFY in-game]`
> and let the Guru confirm from your stat sheet before you target them.

## How guild progression works in Morrowind

- Each faction has ranks (e.g., Associate → Journeyman → … → Master). Advancing
  needs (1) the right number of completed quests and (2) skill requirements.
- **Exact required skill values per rank are game data**; the Guru can help
  you reach them via training/books but should never state a fake threshold.
- Defy a superior, kill an innocent, or break a guild rule (e.g., necromancy
  in the Mages Guild) and you can be expelled. Expulsion isn't permanent for
  most guilds, but avoid it.
- You can be in ALL guilds simultaneously — do them for training access, then
  leave the two you won't build (rarely worth it; expelling early is cheaper
  to undo).

## Major factions

### Mages Guild
- Chapters: Balmora, Caldera, Ald'ruhn, Vivec, Sadrith Mora (Wolverine Hall).
- **Access to** the Guide-travel network + cheap trainers of all magic skills
  + daedra-killing quests (soul gems/resources).
- Good early mage/fighter/multi all-rounder guild; two necromancy-related
  quests can conflict — choose the "clean" route to stay friendly.
- Note: the Mages Guild questline has a "steal-then-return" branch for the
  Guildmaster spot (Trebonius). `[VERIFY]` exact quest names before relying.

### Fighters Guild
- Chapters everywhere; fastest gold + straightforward "clear X" quests.
- The classic early money engine: the Balmora Fighters Guild quests.
  `[VERIFY]` chapter-leader names before relying on them.
- Highest ranks need real combat skills + reputation. Great for melee.

### Thieves Guild
- Centers on Balmora (Sugar-Lips Habasi) + Vivec's arena district.
- Fences, easy gold, Open/Chameleon skill synergy. The "Vivec informant"
  sub-questline weaves with the main quest's Mehra Milo arc — do it in sync.

### Morag Tong (legal assassin guild)
- Formula of silk: honor-bound, VISIBLE assassinations; Morag Tong Writ
  protects you from Imperial guards. Strong skill/fatigue/stealth perks.

### Fighters/Mages/Thieves "triple membership"
- Morrowind does **not** forbid holding membership in all three mainstream
  guilds at once. Use them for trainer/teleport/gold access, then let rank
  rise only in the guild that fits your build.

### Tribunal Temple
- Faith-based, Vivec City. Restoration/Alchemy/Mysticism affinity. The
  "Divine and Almsivi Intervention" relic synergy. Pilgrimage quests teach
  the island. Canonical Nerevarine flavor.

### Imperial Cult
- Underwhelming rewards early but easy levels + strong trainers. Good for
  quick rep and the "50 reputation skip" main-quest shortcut (05).

### Imperial Legion
- Combat military quests guarding forts; Reputation gate keeps ranks until
  you're famous. Endgame-ish for warriors.

### Great Houses (major choice — doing 2 is tedious, pick 1)
- **House Redoran** (Ald'ruhn, Honor/Warrior) — melee; stronghold missions;
  main-quest Hortator arc dovetails.
- **House Hlaalu** (Balmora/Vivec, commerce) — mercantile/speech; stronghold
  in the western plantation lands; easiest money.
- **House Telvanni** (Tel Aruhn / Sadrith Mora, wizard-mercants) — magic
  supremacists; quests include failed experiments, conjuring, and giant
  mushrooms (their strongholds are in towers). Highest magic synergy.
- Joining a Great House is a long commitment; joining 2 ruins rep. Join the
  one matching your combat style (Redoran melee / Hlaalu money / Telvanni
  mage). `[VERIFY]` house-joining prerequisites per house.

## Rank-up efficiency rules

1. **Know the skill requirement before questing.** Ex: to advance in a
   guild you typically need 1-2 skills at a stated level. Plan training so
   quest count and skill count arrive at the same time (otherwise you overdo
   quests with no promotion).
2. **Read the book, get the quest.** Each guild chapter gives you a faction
   "duties" dialogue and the Guild Guide explains who does what.
3. Guild quests also feed the **main-quest "50 reputation" shortcut** and
   the Hortator arcs — do guilds while the MQ waits.
4. When a guild sends an "optional" daedra/temple trap, the Guru should say
   "you can take it now, but it greets you with high loot" so you choose the
   right level to fight it.

## Expansions (GOTY) — when to trigger

- **Tribunal** storyline starts on its own once you're a certain level
  (assassins visit you; city of Mournhold / Clockwork City content). It is
  balanced HOT — don't feel forced; content is meant for mid-to-high level.
- **Bloodmoon** begins via a teleport offer at the Silt Strider/dock at Khuul
  (Boat to Solstheim) — designed significantly-hide; winter survival + werewolf
  content. Recommended level ~15-20+.
- Both expansions keep the base game fully playable; the Guru treats them as
  off-ramps for when the main island feels stale.

## The "10-second faction plan" per build

- **Melee**: Redoran or Imperial Legion + Fighters Guild; Temple for healing.
- **Rogue**: Hlaalu + Thieves Guild + Morag Tong.
- **Mage**: Telvanni + Mages Guild (+ Imperial Cult rep for the shortcut).
- **Balanced**: Hlaalu (money) + Fighters Guild + whichever magic guild fits,
  then House Redoran main-quest Hortator support.
