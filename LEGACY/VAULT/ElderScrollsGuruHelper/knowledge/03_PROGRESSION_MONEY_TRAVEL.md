# 03 — PROGRESSION: MONEY, TRAVEL, GROWTH LOOP

> Practical "how do I keep advancing" — cash, getting around, and levelling
> cleanly. Tags as in 00_ACCURACY_DOCTRINE.md.

## The core growth loop

1. Take a deliverable or kill quest (guild or local) → gold + reputation.
2. Loot everything with decent weight-to-value; vend to the right buyers.
3. Spend gold on **training** (fastest skill growth) and **items** (enchant-cast
   items) at the right vendors — not on overpriced gear from random shops.
4. Rest to bank levels (see 02 for multiplier discipline).
5. Repeat, escalating to harder zones (daedric shrines, dwemer ruins,
   later: Tribunal/Bloodmoon content — see faction/quest files).

Sell prices and disposition: Merchant prices shift with disposition (Personality
+ Speechcraft effect + Mercantile + alchemy bribes). Fatigue affects Mercantile
chance too — full-Fatigue before haggling.

## Money: the verified fast paths

- **Creeper** `[VERIFIED]`: a Scamp merchant on the top floor of **Ghorak
  Manor**, **Caldera** (enter Caldera, Ghorak Manor is a large building). He
  ignores disposition and buys at near-full price, with a large gold pool
  (`~5,000`  [VERIFY exact]). The classic "sell your big loot here" hub.
- **Mudcrab Merchant** `[VERIFY exact islet]`: a talking mudcrab on a small
  island in the shallows off Vivec's west coast; similar no-haggle buyer with
  a bigger pool (`~10,000` [VERIFY exact]). Combine both — sell to whichever
  has gold left.
- **Stolen goods**: sell stolen loot to any merchant who takes it (Thieves
  Guild fences, or just to a "discount merchant" — most merchants buy stolen
  loot if the disposition/barter succeeds); the Creeper/Mudcrab also buy
  stolen items. Keeps you clear as long as you aren't caught.
- **Quest gold + notoriety**: Fighters Guild and Temple/Imperial Cult quests
  pay and give Reputation (see 04). Reputation opens faction rewards and the
  main-quest shortcut (see 05).
- **Trivially profitable start quests** around Seyda Neen/Balmora: several
  short deliveries and the "stolen items from **Arrille's Tradehouse**" style
  fetch-and-sell quest of Fargoth's ring (a ring + disposition + the loot from
  the hiding spot). Low risk, teaches the barter/bribe loop.
- **Training cost control**: pay trainers at lower skill levels or via the
  Drain Skill trick (02) — the same gold buys more skill points.

## Getting around Vvardenfell (fast travel network)

- **Silt Striders (land)** — bug rides between the settled land towns.
  Confirmed stops include **Seyda Neen, Balmora, Caldera, Ald'ruhn, Gnisis,
  Maar Gan, Khuul, Molag Mar, Suran**; `[VERIFY]` full route list before
  relying on a specific hop. No strider at Vivec city itself.
- **Boats** — coastal towns: **Seyda Neen, Ebonheart, Vivec, Sadrith Mora,
  Tel Aruhn, Tel Mora, Khuul, Molag Mar/Hla Oad**-area and out to **Dagon
  Fel**; also **Khuul → Solstheim** for Bloodmoon. `[VERIFY]` per route.
- **Mages Guild Guides** `[VERIFY full set]` — instant teleport between
  connected guild halls; Balmora, Caldera, Ald'ruhn, Vivec, Wolverine Hall
  (Sadrith Mora) are the core set. Cheap, instant, and atronach-friendly
  (teleporting refills you nothing — absorb against another mage instead).
- **Mark & Recall + Interventions (Mysticism)** `[VERIFIED]`: `Mark`/`Recall`
  pins one spot anywhere; `Divine Intervention` (to nearest Imperial shrine)
  and `Almsivi Intervention` (to nearest Temple) are cheap escape/reposition
  tools. Buy or find these low-level scrolls/spells early and keep them.
- **Propylon chambers (Dwemer)** `[VERIFY]`: but you still want the indices /
  though most start broken; not a priority network.

Movement note: **Weight, not height, affects run speed** (heavier races move
faster at same Speed), and Speed + Athletics + accumulated movement is your
main time-saver. Boost Speed via the Steed sign or Speed-leveling choices
rather than grinding Athletics alone.

## Gear progression guide

- Armor: early **Chitin/Bonemold** (Light/Medium) are cheap and plentiful in
  Dunmer areas; **Steel/Imperial** for Heavy. Mid: **Dreugh/Orcish/Bonemold
  heavy**, later Glass (Light, great if enchant-loaded), Ebony, Daedric.
  `[VERIFY]` exact tier names per armor skill — Morrowind has Light/Medium/
  Heavy with distinct sets.
- Weapons: **Silver is mandatory for spectral undead** (ghosts, wraiths,
  lich) — normal-steel weapons do almost nothing to them until you have
  silver/enchanted/daedric. Keep a silver weapon, a silver arrow on a bow,
  or an enchant-cast weapon as an anti-ghost kit. `[VERIFIED]-ish`
- Enchanted items: cast-on-use daggers / rings for utility spells you don't
  have skill to cast (e.g., a `Reflect` or `Fortify` ring) beat raw skill
  investment early.

## Early "open the world" checklist (any build)

1. Buy **Mark/Recall** (or the scrolls) + a **Divine/Almsivi Intervention**.
2. Get a **soul trap + a cast-enchant weapon or scroll** once Grand/Common
   souls start dropping (see 07 enchanting).
3. Claim the **free starter cash/armor** from the Fighters/Mages/Thieves guild
   questlines (04) instead of buying gear.
4. Join **one Great House** when you hit ~level 5-8 and its favored playstyle,
   because house ranks unlock strongholds and traders (04).
5. Ignore the main quest's urgency — Caius Cosades explicitly tells you to
   train and build before returning; Morrowind's MQ has **no time limit**.
   Go side-content when efficiency demands.
