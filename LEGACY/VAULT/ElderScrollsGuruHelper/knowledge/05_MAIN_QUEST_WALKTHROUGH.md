## Act 0 — Starting out (no combat pressure)

- Arrive on the **Imperial Prison Ship** at **Seyda Neen**. In the
  **Census and Excise Office**, finish the release-math, take your package
  (it's the one Caius Cosades needs), and exit town.
- Return the optional side errands here for easy loot/rep later — but the
  main thread is: get to **Balmora** and hand the package to
  **Caius Cosades** (the **South Wall Cornerclub**, Balmora).
- No time limit. Caius explicitly tells you to rank up before serious quests.

## Act 1 — The Blades / Caius Cosades research chain

Caius sends you to gather intelligence on the Nerevarine cult and the lost
prophecy. The informant quests (in any order):

1. **Antabolis Informant** — **Hasphat Antabolis**, Fighters Guild, Balmora.
2. **Gra-Muzgob Informant** — **Sharn gra-Muzgob**, Mages Guild, Balmora
   (her info is lore-tentative, keep digging).
3. **Vivec Informants** — three informants across Vivec city.
4. **Zainsubani Informant** — **Hassour Zainsubani**, Ald'ruhn (Ashlander
   trader; lead to the Urshilaku camp).
5. Field missions tied to this chain (UESP numbered sub-quests): the Dwemer
   ruin **Arkngthand** (near Balmora), the **Andrano Ancestral Vault**, and
   the trip out to **Vivec** + **Ashlander Informant** at Ald'ruhn.

## Act 2 — Caius' orders + the Corprus hook

1. **Meet Sul-Matuul** at the **Urshilaku Camp** (Ashlander camp, far NW
   coast near the Foyada toward the sea; reachable via Silt Strider ×2
   routes or an overland trek).
   - You must prove yourself: Sul-Matuul's chain (the Bone-Biter bow
     retrieval) or the "tribute" dialogue gate (200-gold `[VERIFY]` exact)
     lets you into the camp's inner circle and Nibani's yurt.
2. **Sixth House Base** near **Gnaar Mok** — cleared on Caius' order; expect
   Sixth House Sleepers/corpus cultists.
3. **Corprus Cure** — this is where the MQ gets grim: being touched by
   corprus during the above makes Caius send you to **Tel Fyr** for the
   Corprusarium treatment. Spoiler: it changes your character permanently
   (and to full advantage eventually). Do what the dome-dweller says.

## Act 3 — The Lost Prophecies (gating act)

- **Mehra Milo and the Lost Prophecies** — **Mehra Milo**, Temple Canton,
  Vivec (Temple sect; links to the Thieves Guild's Vivec informant arc).
- **The Path of the Incarnate** — **Nibani Maesa**, wise woman, Urshilaku
  Camp. She reads the trials you must pass and sets the recognition stages.

## Act 4 — The Seven Trials (the "open world" act)

Two chains run concurrently, any order:

**Become Hortator — 3 Great Houses:**
- Redoran Hortator (Ald'ruhn, council votes + a duel in Vivec's Arena).
- Hlaalu Hortator (Balmora/Vivec council votes, the "plantation" testimony).
- Telvanni Hortator (Sadrith Mora — the Telvanni council, plus the
  "failed experiment" resolution).
`[VERIFY]` per-house councilor list before Wizardly blunders.

**Become Nerevarine — 4 Ashlander tribes:**
- Urshilaku (the camp you already know), Ahemmusa (NW Grazelands),
  Zainab (East), Erabenimsun (Molag Amur / Red Mountain foothills).
- Each has a trial mission: shrine-clearing, guardian-killing, and escort to
  an inner shrine; completing all four names you Nerevarine with
  the **Moon-and-Star** (your mark). The **Urshilaku first** is recommended.

**Skip-trials shortcut `[VERIFIED]`:** if your character is **level 20+ AND
has 50+ reputation**, you can skip the Fourth (Hortator) and Fifth
(Nerevarine) trials entirely and go straight to the end-game gate.

## Act 5 — End game (spoiler-gated)

> Stop here unless Drew is ready for end-game spoilers.

- **Hortator and Nerevarine**: bring your status to the **Archcanon** in
  Vivec's High Fane → the path to the citadel opens.
- **Acquire the tools** (Vivec lays out the route; there is an alternate
  route via **Yagrum Bagarn / Wraithguard** if Vivec is killed `[VERIFY]`
  alternate). You'll need **Wraithguard** (the gauntlet that lets you handle
  the tools), **Sunder**, and **Keening**.
- **Ash Vampires `[VERIFIED]`**: destroy the seven ash vampires named for
  the citadel's gatekeepers before the final assault.
- **Assault Dagoth Ur** — the citadel under **Red Mountain**, with the
  **Heart of Lorkhan** as the goal. Make sure the gloves are on before.

## Efficiency notes for the whole arc

- Guild/Faction rep feeds the **+50 reputation** and high-level shortcut —
  so do guilds early and get strong before the trials. Caius sends you out
  to train for exactly this reason.
- Keep **Divine/Almsivi Intervention** scrolls equipped while inside sixth
  house/dwemer dungeons: they're the escape hatch that prevents lost loot.
- The **Moon-and-Star ring** and the **Corprus immunity** are permanent
  upsides; don't view the corprus scare as a nerf once the cure is applied.
